## Company

- Berlin-based online fashion and lifestyle platform. Started as an online shoe store in 2008 and serves customers in 25 countries.
- Multi-brand retailer and marketplace with more than 6,000 brands, plus its own private-label brands.
- Product range: clothing, shoes, accessories, beauty, home, and sports goods.
- Listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange.
- Engineering site: <https://engineering.zalando.com>.
- Employee count and revenue vary by source. Use the company's own reports for current figures.

## Tech Academy

Zalando's internal training centre for tech roles. Based on job postings and the careers site, not official curriculum pages:

- Runs monthly tech onboarding programs and tailored learning activities.
- Supports learning culture through internal tech conferences and community events.
- Offers training modules, mentorship, 1:1 and group coaching, and leadership programs.
- Uses a training platform and biannual peer-to-peer reviews.
- A 2022 L&D posting also mentions functional training, train-the-trainer, and ambassador programs. It is dated.
