# AI DevCon NYC

**Dates:** November 2-4, 2026

**Location:** 5-2-A at Industry City, 33 35th St 2nd FL, Brooklyn, NY 11232, USA

**Description:**
A three-day conference focused on agentic coding and AI-native development. The event brings together developers and engineering leaders to explore real-world AI engineering, practical dev tooling, and hands-on sessions.

**Schedule:**

- Monday, Nov 2 -- Workshop Day (in-person only)
- Tuesday, Nov 3 -- Conference talks, demos, sponsor booths, Agentic Genius Bar, and evening party (in-person + virtual)
- Wednesday, Nov 4 -- Conference talks, demos, sponsor booths, Agentic Genius Bar (in-person only)

### Industry Event or Tessl Fan Event?

Assessment: **Tessl-hosted industry event, not a fan event.**

- **Industry signals:** speakers from Google, Google DeepMind, Microsoft/GitHub, AWS, Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, Atlassian, Datadog, Booking.com, Zalando, JetBrains, Sonar, Jellyfish, Harvard, and Queen's University. Independent voices (Steve Yegge, Geoffrey Huntley, Erik Meijer, Bryan Finster, Birgitta Boeckeler, Ray Myers) include critics ("Dark Factory Considered Harmful"). The CFP bans product pitches.
- **Tessl signals:** Tessl hosts, 7 of 66 speakers are Tessl staff, Tessl opens the keynote slot, and the "software factory," "context," and "skills" framing is Tessl's thesis. 11 of 66 speakers (about 1 in 6) work for Tessl, LaunchDarkly, or Port.
- **Implication for me:** content is practitioner-heavy enough to be worth attending, and the Tessl framing is something to listen for, not assume. Hallway and Genius Bar time is where the independent voices are most reachable.

### Expected Attendance

Tessl claims 700+ attendees. That figure is marketing, not a count, and the same page says "Sold out now." For scale: London drew 500+ onsite; 2025 NYC is unpublished. With a 66-speaker, multi-room program, a realistic expectation is several hundred in person, with a free virtual audience on Nov 3.
