# AI DevCon NYC

**Dates:** November 2-4, 2026

**Location:** 5-2-A at Industry City, 33 35th St 2nd FL, Brooklyn, NY 11232, USA

**Description:**
A three-day conference focused on agentic coding and AI-native development. The event brings together developers and engineering leaders to explore real-world AI engineering, practical dev tooling, and hands-on sessions.

**Schedule:**

- Monday, Nov 2 -- Workshop Day (in-person only)
- Tuesday, Nov 3 -- Conference talks, demos, sponsor booths, Agentic Genius Bar, and evening party (in-person + virtual)
- Wednesday, Nov 4 -- Conference talks, demos, sponsor booths, Agentic Genius Bar (in-person only)

**Official Website:** aidevcon.io
