## Live-note summary (2026-10-25)

Source: spoken debrief in a live note, transcribed automatically. Speaker attribution and some names are uncertain (a mic labels everything with the mic owner's name). Verify before relying on it.

### What happened

- A participant ("she") was described as a seeker: not just reading the right books and going to the right workshops, but hungry, doing a daily practice, wanting to go deeper. She got something deeper in one day than in decades of searching.
- The workshop got into confronting territory ("getting in her field is confronting"): past pleasantries toward real sweat. Contrast drawn between putting on leotards for the gym and coming home tired.
- "Dr. Bell" (name uncertain in transcript) was excited to come, loves Michael, works in a similar field, but had vertigo and deep pain the night before and the morning of. Read as a subconscious "no" pushing back on the very thing he wanted.
- Contrast with "woo woo" (candles, gongs, blankies): described as deck chairs. Not dismissing it, but it is limited and boxed.

### What I think I did

- Marketing last month was too nice: "come live in more love and you'll have more freedom." That works for people already willing to live, work, and heal full out; it is not for the average person.
- Even with "sacred love" in every ad, people picked up the intensity subconsciously.

### What I think I should do

1. Be more vociferous and overt about the "how" (practice and experience, not books, videos, or teachers' slogans). Stop pussyfooting.
2. Stop dulling the knife; be incisive. Humility includes saying "I have the how."
3. Call it an intensive, not a workshop: intense, fiery. If you want to do the work, great; otherwise enjoy the sunny day.
4. Follow up with the participant ("she may do more with me or she may not").
5. Verify who "Dr. Bell" is and whether to follow up.

## Insight (2026-10-25 morning)

Source: same spoken debrief; auto-transcribed, wording condensed.

- I have been hiding my real voice. For 31 years I kept separate conversations (Christian, post-Christian, pre-Christian, psychic) and softened the message to be acceptable and heard.
- I told myself that saying "Jesus" or "psychic" would turn off half the people. That leaves almost no one, and almost no one is who I need to talk to.
- I spent a lot of time and money advertising what I thought everybody would want, while hiding what was really happening underneath. People are psychic, so hiding does not work anyway.
- What I can say plainly: direct experience of Jesus (not church), and "psychic" meaning soul, going below everyday consciousness.
- Move: stop hiding and say what I really think, as I would to a close friend. Be intense on purpose: "If you don't want intense, don't come."
