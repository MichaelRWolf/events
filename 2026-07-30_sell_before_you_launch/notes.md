## Announcement

Join Chris Morrison, founder of ViaVerus and creator of the SellNow™ Method, and Emil Runge, Director of Launch Chapel Hill, for a 30-minute conversation about why deep tech founders need to start selling long before launch -- and what the SellNow Boot Camp, delivered this spring with Launch Chapel Hill's accelerator (Launch, powered by KPMG), is proving about early sales discipline.

In 30 minutes, we'll cover:

- Why selling early in the product life cycle changes a startup's trajectory
- What founders in the Spring 2026 SellNow Boot Camp cohort are learning -- and early success factors from the program
- How the Extended Cohort Program keeps founders moving with ongoing mentoring after the boot camp ends
- Why every incubator and accelerator working with deep tech startups -- AI, medtech, materials science, manufacturing, solar, EV -- should consider structured sales development for technical founders

Whether you're a founder trying to land your first customer or a program director looking to show measurable value to your cohort, this conversation is for you.
